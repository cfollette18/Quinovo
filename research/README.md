# Quinovo research

This folder is the written kernel for Quinovo: an open-source operational ontology in the Palantir sense (not a knowledge graph, not a semantic layer).

Read in this order:

1. [taught-with-a-package.md](taught-with-a-package.md) — one teaching pack (not the product) so every layer has a concrete story.
2. [inference.md](inference.md) — graph is storage; inference is the product (deduce, predict, classify, HITL).
3. [plan.md](plan.md) — what we build, in what order, and what we refuse.
4. [agents.md](agents.md) — Hermes, Google ADK, MCP: Quinovo is the world, they are the brains.
5. [framework.md](framework.md) — kernel for anyone; homelab is one pack / dogfood.
6. [ontology-overview.md](ontology-overview.md) — Palantir primitives (Language / Engine / Toolchain).
7. [layers.md](layers.md) — L0–L11 vs what Palantir ships.
8. [cut-line.md](cut-line.md) — what to copy, skip, and replace.
9. [graphify-timesfm.md](graphify-timesfm.md) — plugins, not the product.
10. [acceptance.md](acceptance.md) — sentences a v0 must make true.
11. [sources.md](sources.md) — Foundry docs.

Quinovo is domain-neutral. The homelab is a pack, not the engine.

**Trademarks.** Palantir, Foundry, Gotham, AIP, OSDK, and Ontology Manager are Palantir’s. This research describes their public architecture so Quinovo can implement the *jobs*, not clone product names.
